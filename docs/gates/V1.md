## Fixes required

- **F1** Partial month = a month in which a satellite has fewer acquisition days than
  days in the month (per satellite: Terra, Aqua, S-NPP, NOAA-20), computed by the
  pipeline over the whole input. Rebuild, recompute R and K.
- **F2** Docs: method.md 4.1 precedent wording; 4.4 and 4.5 state what happens after the
  archive ends; caveat 2 states the extrapolation to high-fire years and the drift; R
  shown as per-month values when per-month R applies.
- **F3** CSV: estimated values to 1 decimal at export; ratios to 3 decimals; sums use
  full precision.
- **F4** UI wording: "scaled to VIIRS-equivalent (MODIS x R)" rather than "harmonized"
  for estimated values (web owner).

## Open, decided later

- R fit window: physicist proposes ending the R overlap at 2021-12, before MODIS orbit

- Sao Paulo cane-burning phase-out (2002-2017) may bias the backcast in that area (lead,

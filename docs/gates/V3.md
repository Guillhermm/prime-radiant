## Fixes required

- **L1** Replace d/N with a 12 x 31 table of mean cumulative within-month shares from the
  national S-NPP daily data 2012-2025 (leave-one-year-out bias at most 4% for Brazil);
  generated by a script from `raw/country`, committed as a small table with its command.
  Small AOIs (Pantanal: 25-30% off either way) get a low-confidence label.
- **L2** A day is partial when its global record count is under 50% of the window median;
  a partial day is null, the month-to-date ends at the last complete day, and the fetch
  day never counts. The first day is not partial by default.
- **L3** The live series is NOAA-20 x K (S-NPP units) for the whole live period; core
  checks the unit the caller declares and refuses MODIS without x R.
- **L4** The band uses VIIRS-era years only (V2 decision).
- **L5** Live values are multiplied by the archive's type-0 share for that calendar month
  (assumption, stated), or the bias is stated per month on screen.
- **L6** Wording: "approximate", name the sensor (NOAA-20 scaled to S-NPP), NRT, the
  unfiltered bias, the last complete day; S-NPP has ended; FIRMS says an Earthdata Login
  is required (fetch must fail visibly if enforced); poll at most hourly; "the archive
  product follows after a 2-3 month lag" (FIRMS FAQ).

For the web owner: `api/live.js` counts a fire-cell-day from the union of NOAA-20,
NOAA-21 and MODIS NRT, which is not comparable to the single-sensor band and includes
NOAA-21, which the archive excludes.

Sources: FIRMS FAQ, https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms/faq; FIRMS page,
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms; NASA CMR records for MCD14DL C6.1,
VNP14IMGTDL_NRT and VJ114IMGTDL_NRT.

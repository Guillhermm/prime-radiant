## Fixes required

- **A1** S-NPP partial months from 2018 on are supplied by NOAA-20 x K (per-month K),
  flagged `estimated`, instead of the undercounted observed value (also closes the V1
  open caveat).
- **A2** Flag rule: p = (count of earlier values >= v, plus 1) / (n + 1); flagged when
  p <= 0.1 and n >= 9 (false-flag rate at most 10% for every n; ties count against).
  The rank (0-1) stays as the displayed value.
- **A3** Single-sensor confirmation: a flag shows only when the same rule also holds on
  MODIS alone (2002-07 on) or on VIIRS alone (n >= 9); otherwise the month is labeled
  `cross-era` (rank shown, not flagged).
- **A4** Year rank: rank on two totals, observed and partial months scaled by days in
  month over days observed (with A1 applied); a year is ranked when both give the same
  rank, else shown as a range with its reason.
- **A5** Flags with a median-0 earlier pool carry `sparse-baseline`.
- **A6** Wording: "percentile rank against the same month in earlier years (50% =
  typical)"; caveats for small n (at 9-10 years a flag means a record or near-record),
  scaled years before 2012, sparse areas, and a rank relative to this archive, not a
  standard climatology. MODIS x R months after 2021 (orbit drift) are ranked with the
  `estimated` caveat.

Sources: JRC/FIRMS fire anomaly layer (Africa Knowledge Platform); INPE Infoqueima
bulletin, June 2026 (baseline 2010-2024); Zhang et al. 2005, J. Climate 18, 1641-1651,
doi:10.1175/JCLI3366.1 (in-base percentile discontinuities; abstract only); Libonati et
al. 2022, Environ. Res. Lett. 17, 015005 (Pantanal 2020; cited from memory, not fetched).

Rule decided after one relay round (the two roles swapped positions; the main session
chose): flag = harmonized p <= 0.1 and (MODIS alone or VIIRS alone p <= 0.1), n >= 9;
`confirmedBy` records the sensor; non-flag label `viirs-era-record` when VIIRS alone
(outage months filled by NOAA-20 x K included) is a record but the harmonized test fails.
Brazil: 6 flags (2016-03, 2016-04, 2019-03, 2021-05, 2022-05, 2024-03), viirs-era-record
on 2021-06, 2022-06, 2023-06, 2024-06, 2024-08. Pantanal: 9 flags, none.

Caveats that stay: a count rank is not a burned-area rank (large intense fires saturate
counts; Pantanal 2024 ranks 5-6 by count but 2nd by burned area since 2012, LASA via
press); MODIS x R probably overestimates 2002-2007, so the harmonized test errs toward
fewer flags; the anomaly rank is relative to this archive, not a standard climatology.

## Fixes required

- **A1** S-NPP partial months from 2018 on are supplied by NOAA-20 x K (per-month K),
  flagged `estimated`, instead of the undercounted observed value (also closes the V1
  open caveat).
- **A2** Flag rule: p = (count of earlier values >= v, plus 1) / (n + 1); flagged when
  p <= 0.1 and n >= 9 (false-flag rate at most 10% for every n; ties count against).
  The rank (0-1) stays as the displayed value.
- **A3** Single-sensor confirmation: a flag shows only when the same rule also holds on
  MODIS alone (2002-07 on) or on VIIRS alone (n >= 9); otherwise the month is labeled
  `cross-era` (rank shown, not flagged).
- **A4** Year rank: rank on two totals, observed and partial months scaled by days in
  month over days observed (with A1 applied); a year is ranked when both give the same
  rank, else shown as a range with its reason.
- **A5** Flags with a median-0 earlier pool carry `sparse-baseline`.
- **A6** Wording: "percentile rank against the same month in earlier years (50% =
  typical)"; caveats for small n (at 9-10 years a flag means a record or near-record),
  scaled years before 2012, sparse areas, and a rank relative to this archive, not a
  standard climatology. MODIS x R months after 2021 (orbit drift) are ranked with the
  `estimated` caveat.

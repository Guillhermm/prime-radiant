# Gate G2: static-source mask

Date: 2026-11-15. Claim: `pipeline/static-mask.js`, applied by `pipeline/build.js` to every build, fixes the type filter
that gate G1 rejected outside Brazil. Checked independently by the reviewer, the mathematician and the physicist; the rule
below is the version after their findings.

**Verdict: accepted with caveats.** The thresholds are a team assumption, not a published rule.

## The rule

A first pass over the VIIRS archive files (S-NPP, NOAA-20) builds a set of 0.01 degree cells; the second pass drops
type-0 records of every sensor and year that fall in one (counted as `static_masked` in the build stats). A cell is masked
when:

- it holds, or is one of the 8 neighbors of, a VIIRS archive detection of type 1 (volcano) or 2 (other static land
  source), anywhere, also outside the build's area; or
- VIIRS type-0 detections fall in it on at least 10 distinct UTC days of one year in one file, in at least 2 distinct years.

## What the roles found, and what changed

| Finding | Role | Change |
|---|---|---|
| FIRMS does not flag all static sources: the MODIS C6 guide (7.2.3, 8.7.3) says persistent industrial and volcanic spots are not properly flagged and are not removed from MCD14ML; the VIIRS 375 m guide 1.2 (6.1) says type-2 sources were often labeled 0 in v1/v2, fixed in v3 (May 2025). Our country files are VIIRS version 2 | reviewer | Premise verified |
| No published "10 days in 2 years, 8 neighbors" rule; persistence masking is published in principle (Liu et al. 2018, RSE 204) | reviewer | Thresholds stated as an assumption; sensitivity below |
| Type 3 also marks fires over inland water and wetlands: seeding with it removed 10,766 detections in one 1 degree box of the 2024 Pantanal season | physicist | Type 3 left out |
| A 0.01 degree cell suits VIIRS (375 m) but not MODIS (up to about 2 x 4.8 km off nadir) | physicist | MODIS seeds nothing (also: MODIS v03 type 2 covers about 81,000 such cells in Brazil, with the seasonal cycle of the fires, so it marks fire areas) |
| Two CSV entries repeating the same days were counted twice; an undated file put two years in one; neighbors did not wrap at 180 degrees; typed sources just outside the area did not mask inside it | mathematician | All four fixed, with tests (`pipeline/static-mask.test.js`) |

## Measurements

Share of type-0 detections masked, VIIRS S-NPP 2021-2024 country files (persistence only, by minimum days a year, 2 years):

| Area | 5 days | 10 days | 20 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angola | 0.6% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| Nigeria | 0.9% | 0.4% | 0.2% |
| Punjab (agricultural burning) | 0.8% | 0.2% | 0.2% |
| Brazil | 4.7% | 0.2% | 0.0% |
| Brazil, arc of deforestation | 9.2% | 0.4% | 0.0% |
| Hassi Messaoud (flares) | 53% | 44% | 41% |
| Rumaila (flares) | 61% | 48% | 35% |
| Niger Delta | 14% | 9.5% | 5.6% |

10 days is the knee: below it savanna and deforestation cells go, above it flares stay. With the typed seeds added, the
whole rule removes, of 2024 type-0 detections: Algeria 56%, Iraq 21%, Nigeria 0.9%, Angola 0.03%, Brazil 0.7%.
Brazil full archive build (2000-2025, local, before and after): see the table in `docs/data.md`, "Static-source mask".

## Caveat shown to users

Gas flares, volcanoes and industrial heat sources are removed using FIRMS source types and multi-year persistence. Some
flares may remain (new ones, ones lit on fewer than 10 days a year, MODIS detections spread over several cells), and a
spot about 1 km across that burns on 10 or more days a year, in two years, is dropped.

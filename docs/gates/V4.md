# Gate V4: verdict (fire outlook, experimental)

Date: 2026-11-15. Claim: the experimental fire outlook, commit fbaca5a: `core/outlook.js`,
`pipeline/outlook.js`, `core/data/outlook-model.json`, `docs/method.md` section 7. A
logistic regression per 1 deg cell gives the chance that next month's VIIRS-era
fire-cell-days land above the cell's same-month median of earlier years, from our own
fire history only. Checked independently by the mathematician and the physicist.

**Verdict: rejected as is; accepted once fixes O1-O5 land.** The model has real but
small skill over a calibrated persistence forecast. As built it shows a forecast that
is three months stale.

| Item | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Reproduction | accepted | Mathematician's independent refit (Newton) matches exactly: 22,393 train and 27,832 test samples, Brier 0.2178, AUC 0.7074, same coefficients |
| Leakage | accepted | Every feature uses month t or earlier; medians and climatology use earlier years only (K uses all years, negligible) |
| Baselines and skill claim | **rejected** | Shrunk climatology (Brier 0.261) is worse than a constant 0.5 (0.250), and persistence was scored as 0/1. Against calibrated persistence (Brier 0.2269) BSS is 0.040 (tile x year 95% CI 0.020-0.057), not 0.370; against 0.5 it is 0.129 (0.079-0.192). Anomaly alone reaches Brier 0.221 |
| Calibration | accepted with caveats | p >= 0.8 over by 0.042 (not significant); p < 0.2 under by 0.04 |
| Median-0 cells and sparse cells | accepted with caveats | 20% of test cell-months excluded; skill about 0 for median <= 1, 0.21 for median > 30 |
| Training span | accepted with caveats | 3 training years (2018-02 on), not 4; base rate not stationary (intercept -0.54 to -0.17) |
| Coefficients | accepted with caveats | Current anomaly dominates (0.47 standardized), then previous year and 3-month mean; damped persistence; cannot see rain onset, ENSO or policy changes, so it misses turning points |
| 2026-08 check | accepted with caveats | Against MODIS August 2026: Brier 0.156, AUC 0.79, direction right, underconfident low; one month only, not a skill test |
| Timeliness | **rejected as framed** | The archive VIIRS ends 2026-07, so on 2026-11-15 the "next month" is 2026-08: a stale hindcast |
| Comparators | accepted | INPE Risco de Fogo and CEMS/GWIS fire danger use weather; ours has none, so it must not be called fire risk or a fire forecast |

## Fixes required

- **O1** Report skill against a constant base rate and a calibrated persistence (P(above
  given this month above), fit on training), with tile x year block-bootstrap 95% CIs;
  drop the shrunk climatology as the headline baseline.
- **O2** Cells with median 0 or median <= 1 carry `no-skill` (shown, labeled, not ranked).
- **O3** Forecast from the latest complete month: feed the near-real-time months
  (NOAA-20 x K, type-0 share, partial days handled as in `core/live.js`) after the archive
  ends; if a month is not available, label the output a hindcast with its months.
- **O4** Every output carries the target month and the last observed month.
- **O5** Docs: 3 training years; the required UI wording: "Experimental. Chance that next
  month's hotspot activity in this 1 deg cell is above its usual (median) level, learned
  only from past satellite hotspot counts. No rainfall, weather or drought data. Not a
  fire-danger rating or a count of fires. For fire danger, see INPE Risco de Fogo or
  GWIS." Never "fire risk" or "fire forecast".

Sources: INPE Risco de Fogo methodology (dataserver-coids.inpe.br, RiscoFogo_Sucinto
2017 and doc_RF_2007); CEMS seasonal fire danger (ewds.climate.copernicus.eu,
cems-fire-seasonal); Di Giuseppe et al. 2024, Sci Data (PMC10810953); GWIS fire danger
forecast technical background.

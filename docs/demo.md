# Demo script (3 minutes)

URL: https://primeradiant.zenus.systems. Say this first, out loud: the data on screen is synthetic, generated by the team to show how the app works. The band at the top says so. The real FIRMS pipeline runs locally but its tiles are not hosted yet (planned), so no count shown is a real detection. The method applied to them is the real one, accepted at science gate V1.

Check before presenting: open the URL once on the venue network and keep the tab loaded (the service worker caches the shell; the tiles need the network on first load).

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the URL. Point at the "Synthetic demo data" band. | The problem: MODIS (since 2000) and VIIRS (since 2012) count fires differently, so one long record is hard to read. Prime Radiant puts both on one calendar. The data here is synthetic. |
| 0:30 | First-run screen: click the Pantanal example area. | One click picks a bounding box. The same box can be typed or drawn on the map. |
| 1:00 | Read the latest-month verdict and the Radiant (the polar year-by-month view). | When does this place burn? The Radiant shows the season shape across years. |
| 1:30 | Calendar tab: point at the year totals, the median row and one hot month. Click it. | Each cell is one month. The selected-month panel names the sensor behind the value: S-NPP as counted, or MODIS scaled to VIIRS-equivalent (MODIS × R) before 2012. Comparisons with the median carry a Preview tag; the science gate has not covered them. |
| 2:00 | Open Method: point at the satellite timeline and the three method steps. | MODIS flies from 2000, S-NPP from 2012, NOAA-20 from 2018. Where MODIS and S-NPP both fly, their ratio R puts MODIS on the VIIRS scale, and months scaled from MODIS are marked estimated. The science team accepted the method at gate V1. |
| 2:30 | Close Method, then Export CSV; the share button on the area chip copies the link. | The CSV carries the numbers on screen, with a first line saying the data is synthetic. Every view is a shareable link. |
| 2:50 | Spoken close, no screen needed (on a phone, scroll back to the top first if you opened Method there). | Real tiles, validated harmonization, anomalies and the live season come next; none is claimed today. |

Fallbacks: if the network is down, the cached shell loads but tiles may not; use `npm run mock && npm run serve` locally. All tabs are on for synthetic data and labeled Synthetic; Anomalies and Live also carry Preview. Sensors shows R and K with their 95% intervals, the same factors the Calendar applies. Live runs on placeholder data (the app does not call the live proxy yet, and production has no `MAP_KEY`): do not demo it as working.
